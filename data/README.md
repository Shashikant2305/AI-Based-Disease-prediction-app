# Training data provenance

`pima_indians_diabetes.csv` is a headerless, comma-separated snapshot of the Pima Indians Diabetes Database. The first eight columns are, in order: pregnancies, 2-hour plasma glucose concentration, diastolic blood pressure, triceps skinfold thickness, 2-hour serum insulin, BMI, diabetes pedigree function, and age. The ninth column is the binary outcome (`0` or `1`).

Source snapshot: [Brownlee Machine Learning Datasets mirror](https://github.com/jbrownlee/Datasets/blob/master/pima-indians-diabetes.data.csv). Dataset notes: [pima-indians-diabetes.names](https://github.com/jbrownlee/Datasets/blob/master/pima-indians-diabetes.names). The bundled CSV's Git blob SHA-1 is `9c3e9b8cce56e51b8e1837ef7bc1a3303f37fb46`. The mirror attributes the original data to the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases and the database donation to Vincent Sigillito, Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory. Its notes identify 768 records, all women aged at least 21 of Pima heritage living near Phoenix, Arizona.

Original publication: Smith, J. W., Everhart, J. E., Dickson, W. C., Knowler, W. C., & Johannes, R. S. (1988). “Using the ADAP learning algorithm to forecast the onset of diabetes mellitus.” *Proceedings of the Symposium on Computer Applications and Medical Care*, 261–265.

The source mirror does not specify a separate license for this dataset. Preserve attribution and verify reuse and redistribution terms before republishing the data. This small, historical, restricted cohort must not be treated as representative or used for clinical decisions.
